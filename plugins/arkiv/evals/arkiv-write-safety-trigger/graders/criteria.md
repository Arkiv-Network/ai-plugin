Serialize one signer and validate rows, measured batch sizes and batch results. Persist prepared/submitting intent before the wallet call and preserve any known transaction hash immediately. Receipt-confirmed journal entries must survive a failed checkpoint or readback; they are distinct from overall verified completion. Before every new signature, run a fresh exact unsigned estimate at latest; stop on estimation errors. For N operations require gas >= max(1_000_000 + 400_000 × (N − 1), 2 × estimate) and gas <= latestBlock.gasLimit / 2, within authorized balance/spend. Split unprepared work when it does not fit; stop rather than silently changing a frozen plan. Allow at most one retry per operation, only after an authoritative reverted receipt proves gas exhaustion (gasUsed === gasLimit) and the SDK did not report success. For that retry obtain a fresh latest estimate, use at least double the previous gas cap while satisfying both gas bounds, and refresh nonce and gasPrice under exclusive signer custody. Never resend after SDK success or an uncertain submission, including the same signed bytes; non-gas reverts are terminal. Preserve hashes, intent and receipt-confirmed progress and repair readback/checkpoints without another send. Verify receipts, matching input/key order and intended fields before declaring the import complete. Report partial progress. No execution may be fabricated. Judge only these requested behaviors.
